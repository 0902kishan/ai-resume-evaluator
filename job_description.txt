Machine Learning Engineer
TechNova Analytics — Bengaluru, India (Hybrid)

About the Role
TechNova Analytics is looking for a Machine Learning Engineer to join our Applied AI team.
You will design, train, and deploy deep learning models that power our recommendation and
forecasting products, working closely with data engineers and product managers to ship
models from research to production.

Responsibilities
- Design and implement deep learning models (CNNs, RNNs, Transformers) using PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Build and maintain data pipelines for training and evaluation at scale
- Optimize model training and inference performance (mixed precision, distributed training)
- Deploy models to production using REST APIs, Docker, and cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP)
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate business problems into ML solutions
- Write clean, well-tested, production-grade Python code
- Track experiments and maintain reproducible ML workflows (MLflow, Weights & Biases)
- Mentor junior engineers and contribute to code reviews

Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field
- 1-3 years of experience building and deploying machine learning models
- Strong proficiency in Python and one deep learning framework (PyTorch preferred)
- Solid understanding of neural network fundamentals, optimization, and regularization
- Experience with SQL and data manipulation libraries (pandas, NumPy)
- Familiarity with version control (Git) and collaborative software development

Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with NLP or computer vision projects
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Familiarity with Docker, Kubernetes, or CI/CD pipelines
- Experience with big data tools (Spark, Hadoop) is a plus
- Contributions to open-source ML projects or published research

What We Offer
- Competitive salary and performance bonuses
- Flexible hybrid work policy
- Learning budget for conferences and certifications
- Health insurance and wellness benefits
